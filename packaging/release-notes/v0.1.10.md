RAWmakase 0.1.10 brings Lightroom's **Upright** to the Transform panel, so both new and imported edits can straighten horizons and converging verticals, and the Transform sliders now match Camera Raw. Auto white balance now lands where Lightroom's does, and the **Auto** button sets tone only, leaving your white balance alone. Lens profiles apply to more camera and lens combinations, compressed Fujifilm and Canon raw files open faster from the Linux packages, and Macs can install and update RAWmakase with Homebrew.

## New

- **Upright.** The Transform panel's **Off**, **Auto**, **Level**, **Vertical** and **Full** buttons now work, laid out in two rows with an **Update** button as in Lightroom. RAWmakase finds straight lines and vanishing points in the photo and corrects for them at the lens's focal length. Presets that set only an Upright mode are analysed for each photo they are applied to. **Guided** and **Constrain Crop** are not available yet.
- **Lightroom's Upright edits open as Lightroom made them.** Photos edited with Upright in Lightroom or Camera Raw, including Guided, were previously rejected on import. They now render as Camera Raw renders them, and switching between modes needs no new analysis. Upright settings are written back when you export to XMP or a catalog.
- **Homebrew for Mac.** Install with `brew install --cask pch/tap/rawmakase` and update with `brew upgrade --cask rawmakase`.

## Improved

- **Auto white balance that matches Lightroom.** **WB → Auto** used to come out noticeably cooler than Lightroom's Auto, sent shade and overcast scenes far too blue, and left tungsten or neon night scenes orange. It now follows Lightroom's Auto closely, and stays within the 2850–7500 K range Lightroom's Auto keeps to. Lightroom edits saved with Auto white balance but no stored temperature use the same estimate.
- **Transform sliders match Camera Raw.** Vertical, Horizontal, Rotate, Aspect, Scale and the offsets now produce the same geometry as Camera Raw, including on photos the camera turned to portrait, where they could previously be far off.
- **Lens profiles for more lenses.** A profile made on another camera make now applies when there isn't one for your camera's make, such as Adobe's Sigma-made profile for a Sigma lens on a Panasonic or Leica body. Profiles from a make sharing your camera's mount are preferred, and full-frame profiles are scaled to smaller sensors. Thanks to [@ilookee](https://github.com/ilookee) for reporting this ([#23](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/issues/23)).
- **Faster opening of compressed Fujifilm and Canon files on Linux.** The Debian, Ubuntu and Fedora packages and the Linux archives now unpack compressed RAF and CR3 files on all CPU cores. On an 8-core Ryzen desktop, 40 MP Fujifilm X100VI files opened about 2.7× faster and Canon PowerShot V1 CR3 files about 1.5× faster; the exported pixels are identical.

## Changed

- **Auto sets tone only.** The Basic panel's **Auto** button (**⌘⇧U / Ctrl+Shift+U**) now sets Exposure, Contrast, Highlights, Shadows, Whites and Blacks for the photo's current white balance, as Lightroom's does, and no longer changes white balance. Use **WB → Auto** for that. The WB menu shows **Auto** while the photo keeps Auto's result and **Custom** once you change it, and pasted settings and presets carry the values as Custom.
- **Transform sliders follow the displayed photo.** As in Lightroom, Vertical and Horizontal act along the photo as you see it. RAWmakase edits made earlier with Transform sliders on portrait photos will look different.

## Fixed

- **The 100% view no longer flickers while panning.** For a frame or two the photo could jump back to where it was before the drag.

## Requirements

Mac downloads require macOS 15+. Windows downloads are for 64-bit Windows 10 and later (x86_64). Linux packages are for x86_64 and ARM (aarch64): Ubuntu 24.04+ or Debian 13+, or Fedora 43+; the Arch Linux package is x86_64. AUR publication remains paused; the Arch package is available directly above. On Apple Silicon Macs and with the Windows installer, 0.1.9 offers this update in the app.
