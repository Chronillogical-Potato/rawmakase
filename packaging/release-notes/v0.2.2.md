RAWmakase 0.2.2 brings the first version of AI masks. In the Masking tool, one click selects the **Subject**, the **Sky** or the **Background**, and you can click the photo to add or remove parts of a selection. Everything runs on your computer and no photo is uploaded. This is an **experimental first version**: it works, but it has clear limits, listed below, and how it works may change. The release also adds Lightroom's panel shortcuts, built previews that open photos faster and let you zoom into offline photos, and fixes for Sony white balance and a Develop panel that could slide off screen.

## TL;DR

- **Select Subject, Sky and Background (experimental).** One-click AI masks that run on your computer, with clicks to refine them.
- **Hide panels as in Lightroom** with Tab, Shift+Tab, F6–F8 or the arrows on the window edges.
- **Build Standard and 1:1 previews.** Photos open with a sharp preview at once, and you can zoom into offline photos.
- **Sony custom white balance matches Camera Raw** on the A7 IV and A7CR in new edits.
- **The Develop panel no longer slides off screen** with long lens or profile names.

## New

- **Select Subject, Sky and Background (experimental)** ([#360](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/360)). In the Masking tool (Shift+W), three new tiles above the Create row make a mask with one click. They are also in each mask's Add, Subtract and Intersect menus.
  - **Subject** selects the people and animals in the photo, up to eight.
  - **Sky** selects the sky, and stops where it ends, so a lake reflecting it is not included.
  - **Background** selects everything except the subject.
  - **Refine with clicks…** lets you click the photo to add to a selection, Alt-click to leave something out, or drag a box to start again from that area. **Regenerate** finds the selection again. Add or Subtract brush components still work for cleanup.
  - When Subject finds no person or animal, **Click the subject…** lets you select any object yourself, with clicks or a box.
  - **On your computer, downloaded only when you ask.** The first time, the drawer asks to download two models (about 270 MB). Nothing downloads on its own. The models run on the CPU, so the first selection on a photo takes a few seconds, and the next ones on that photo are quick. **Remove selection model** deletes them. Masks you have already made keep working without the models.
  - **Needs a catalog upgrade.** Selections are stored in the catalog, so the drawer asks to **Upgrade catalog…** once. It writes a backup copy next to the catalog first. After the upgrade, **RAWmakase 0.2.1 and earlier can't open that catalog**, so close it on your other computers and update them too. New and imported catalogs use the new format from the start. Catalogs you don't upgrade keep working as before.

## About the AI masks

This is a deliberately cautious first version. Please try it on your own photos and [tell us](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/issues) where it gets things wrong.

**Why these models.** We wanted a model that picks "the main subject" of any photo, the way Lightroom's Select Subject does. The best candidate we tested, IS-Net, picked subjects well. However, its weights have no published license, and the data it was trained on may only be used non-commercially. A request to the authors for a commercial license is still unanswered. BiRefNet had the same training-data problem. We only ship models whose license allows it, so RAWmakase combines two Apache-2.0 models instead: **DETR** finds people, animals and sky, and **Segment Anything 2** (Meta) draws their outlines. That works well for what DETR knows, but it can't decide what the subject is in any other photo. If a model with the right license and quality appears, we'll switch to it.

**Known limits:**

- **Subject means people and animals only.** For a car, a building, a product or a sign, it says "No person or animal found". Use **Click the subject…** instead.
- **Hair and fur edges are soft.** The edge is aligned with the photo, but fine strands are not separated the way a dedicated hair model would do it.
- **Sky can miss very dark skies**, or leave small stray patches.
- **No grow/shrink or feather** for these masks yet.
- **Not copied between photos.** Copy, Paste and Sync leave these masks out and tell you so. Presets can't contain them.
- **Catalogs only grow.** Each selection is stored in the catalog, up to a 4096-pixel mask, and old selections are not cleaned up yet.
- **Changing spots, red eye or the mask list during a selection discards the result.** Slider and crop changes don't.
- **Arch Linux:** the Arch package doesn't include ONNX Runtime, the library that runs the models, so the AI masks are unavailable there unless you point `RAWMAKASE_ORT_LIB` at an ONNX Runtime library, version 1.17 or later. All other packages include it.
- **Not measured against Lightroom.** Like the rest of Masking, the results haven't been compared with Lightroom's.

## Also new

- **Hide panels as in Lightroom** ([#346](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/346), [#348](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/348), [#349](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/349)). Tab hides both side panels, and Shift+Tab hides the filmstrip and status bar too. F7, F8 and F6 toggle the left panel, the right panel and the filmstrip on their own. You can also click the thin strips with arrows on the window edges. Each module remembers its layout, also after you restart. The same actions work from scripts, MCP and device buttons.
- **Build Standard-Sized and 1:1 previews** ([#355](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/355), [#357](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/357)). Right-click a thumbnail in the Library or the filmstrip and open **Previews** to build or discard them for the selection. They build in the background, and the top bar shows the progress with a Stop button. Preferences > Catalog sets the Standard size (2048 px by default), shows how much disk previews use, and can discard 1:1 previews automatically after a day, a week or 30 days (the default).
- **Photos open with their preview at once** ([#356](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/356)). When a photo has a Standard preview for its current edit, Develop and the Loupe show it immediately while the photo loads. Before, you saw the camera's uncropped, unedited JPEG for up to two seconds. Previews you built are rebuilt after you edit the photo.
- **Zoom into offline photos.** An offline RAW in the Loupe now shows its Standard preview, and zooms to 100% when it has a 1:1 preview.

## Fixed

- **The Develop panel stays on screen with long lens and profile names** ([#364](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/364)). A long lens name, or a long lens or camera profile name, pushed the right panel partly off the window and cut off its labels. Which photos were affected depended on their lens and the lens profiles you had imported. Long names are now shortened to fit, and you can hover over them to see the full name. Thanks to @nicolasernult-lgtm ([#362](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/issues/362)) and @EttVenter ([#363](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/issues/363)) for the reports.
- **Sony custom white balance matches Camera Raw** ([#361](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/361), [#353](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/issues/353)). On Sony RAWs, a custom Temperature and Tint could look different from Camera Raw even when As Shot matched. RAWmakase now reads each Sony camera's own daylight calibration from the file, since it differs from one unit to the next. This was checked against Camera Raw on the A7 IV and two A7CR bodies. DNG files with supported calibration are now read directly too. This applies to new edits only. White balance you set before looks as it did.

## Requirements

Mac downloads require macOS 15+. Windows downloads are for 64-bit Windows 10 and later (x86_64). Linux packages are for x86_64 and ARM (aarch64): Ubuntu 24.04+ or Debian 13+, or Fedora 43+. The Arch Linux package is x86_64. AUR publication remains paused, so get the Arch package from the link above. Homebrew users update with `brew upgrade --cask rawmakase`. On Apple Silicon Macs and with the Windows installer, 0.2.1 offers this update in the app.

**Catalog compatibility:** a catalog you upgrade for AI masks, and any new or imported catalog, can't be opened by 0.2.1 or earlier. Other catalogs still open in older releases.
