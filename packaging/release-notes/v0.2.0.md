RAWmakase 0.2.0 exports a whole shoot at once. Select photos in the Library or the Filmstrip and export them together, each with its own edit, named with Lightroom's Rename To templates and shown in Finder when they're done. One catalog now works across several computers: each Mac, Linux or Windows machine keeps its own location for the catalog's folders, so a NAS mounted at different paths no longer needs relinking every time you switch. Scripts and AI assistants can straighten photos and list and apply presets. New edits render closer to Camera Raw again: Clarity, color noise reduction, Saturation −100 and the Camera Calibration primaries were each measured against Camera Raw 18.7 and refitted.

## TL;DR

- **Export many photos at once** from the Library or Develop, each with its own edit.
- **Name exports like Lightroom:** sequence numbers, custom names, "x of y" and dates.
- **See your exports when they finish** with After Export › Show in Finder.
- **Use one catalog on several computers**, even when the photos live at different paths on each.
- **Straighten and apply presets from scripts and AI assistants** over the control API and MCP.
- **Closer to Camera Raw:** Clarity, color noise reduction, Saturation −100 and Camera Calibration.

## What you can now do

Shortcuts are shown as on a Mac. On Windows and Linux, use Ctrl for ⌘ and Alt for ⌥.

- **Export a selection:** select photos in the Library and press **⇧⌘E**, or click **Export…** at the foot of the left panel. In Develop, select photos in the Filmstrip, including the open one, and click Export. **⌥⇧⌘E** exports with the previous settings.
- **Keep working while it exports:** the top bar shows **Exporting 24 / 150**. Click it to see the exports waiting and remove one, or click **×** to cancel the running one.
- **Find out what wasn't exported:** if something was skipped, the top bar says so. Click it to open **Problem Exporting Files**, with each photo and the reason.
- **Number or date your files:** Export › **Rename To**, then pick Filename - Sequence, Custom Name - Sequence, Custom Name (x of y) or Date - Filename. Set **Start Number** to begin somewhere other than 1.
- **Open the folder after exporting:** Export › Post-Processing › **After Export** › **Show in Finder** (Show in Explorer on Windows, Show in File Manager on Linux).
- **Take a catalog to another computer:** open it there, then go to Preferences › Catalog › **Folder locations** and click **Change…** for each folder that lives somewhere else on this computer. Your first computer keeps its own locations.
- **Add the same NAS folder on a second computer:** use Add Folder as usual. It joins the folder already in the catalog instead of adding duplicate photos.
- **Straighten from a script or AI assistant:** `rawmakase control set straighten 1.5`, or MCP's `set_parameter` with `straighten` (degrees, −45 to 45). It can also be assigned to a MIDI or Loupedeck dial.
- **Apply a preset from a script or AI assistant:** `rawmakase control presets` lists them and `rawmakase control preset "Name"` applies one. MCP has `list_presets` and `apply_preset` ([MCP guide](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/blob/v0.2.0/docs/mcp.md), [scripting](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/blob/v0.2.0/docs/automation.md)).

## New

- **Batch export.** Export acts on the selection, as in Lightroom. Every photo is exported with its own saved edit, matching what Develop's export gives it pixel for pixel. Exports run one at a time in the background, and existing files are asked about once for the whole export (Overwrite, Skip or Use Unique Names). Before you start, the dialog lists the photos it won't export, such as offline photos or files that aren't raw ([#186](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/186), [#193](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/193)).
- **Lightroom's Rename To templates.** Filename, Filename - Sequence, Filename - Custom Text, Custom Name, Custom Name - Sequence, Custom Name (x of y) and Date - Filename, written as Lightroom's built-in presets write them. Numbers follow the order you chose the photos in, so a photo that fails keeps its number. Dates come from the capture time, and a photo without one is named `undated-…`. Export settings saved with the old "Filename -" checkbox keep their names ([#207](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/207)).
- **After Export: Show in Finder.** Opens one file manager window for each folder exported to, up to five ([#211](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/211)).
- **Folder locations per computer.** Each computer keeps its own location for every folder in a catalog. Preferences › Catalog › Folder locations shows each one, whether it's found, the folders below it that are located separately and the other computers' locations, with Change… and Clear. Folders added on Windows are now found on macOS and Linux too ([#209](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/209)).
- **Straighten and presets over the control API and MCP.** `straighten` (alias `angle`) works with `get`, `set` and `turn`, at 0.1° per tick, and consecutive turns form one History step. `presets` and `preset` list and apply built-in and imported presets as a click in the Presets panel does, and the reply says which settings were skipped ([#208](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/208), [#210](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/210)).

## Improved

- **Rendering closer to Camera Raw.** These were measured against Camera Raw 18.7 and refitted:
  - **Positive Clarity** is local contrast that depends on the size of the detail and on its surroundings, as in Camera Raw, and lands about a third closer to it at +25, +50 and +100 ([#196](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/196)).
  - **Color noise reduction** starts new raw edits at Lightroom's Color 25, Detail 50, Smoothness 50, and removes about as much color noise as Camera Raw while keeping color edges ([#199](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/199)).
  - **Saturation −100** gives each color's own luminance gray, as Camera Raw does, instead of leaving some color. Everything from −50 up is unchanged ([#200](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/200)).
  - **Camera Calibration's Red, Green and Blue Primary** sliders follow Camera Raw's measured changes. On the test chart they went from 1.1–2.0 to 0.62–0.68 ΔE00 ([#192](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/192)).
- **Canon EOS R6 Mark III and PowerShot V1 shadows.** Photos from these bodies no longer open with lifted, washed-out shadows. Both now open within 0.1 EV of Camera Raw ([#195](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/195)).

## Fixed

- **Point Color names the right control.** With Reference tone curves off, Point Color used to tell you to update the process in Calibration, even when there was no Update button. It now says to turn on Reference tone curves, or both when both are needed ([#198](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/198)).

## Changed

- **Rendering changes apply to new edits only.** The new Clarity, color noise reduction, Saturation −100 and Camera Calibration apply to new edits, and to Lightroom edits and presets that set them. Photos you edited before keep the look they had.
- **Saved R6 Mark III and V1 edits brighten their shadows.** The black level fix corrects how these raws are read, so it applies to existing edits too.

## Requirements

Mac downloads require macOS 15+. Windows downloads are for 64-bit Windows 10 and later (x86_64). Linux packages are for x86_64 and ARM (aarch64): Ubuntu 24.04+ or Debian 13+, or Fedora 43+. The Arch Linux package is x86_64. AUR publication remains paused, so get the Arch package from the link above. Homebrew users update with `brew upgrade --cask rawmakase`. On Apple Silicon Macs and with the Windows installer, 0.1.15 offers this update in the app.

Older releases still open a catalog used by 0.2.0, but they ignore per-computer folder locations: they use one location per folder for every computer, as before. Relinking a folder in an older release doesn't move it on a computer that has opened the catalog with 0.2.0. They render edits made in 0.2.0 with their older operators.

Keep a catalog open on one computer at a time. Sharing one live catalog between computers isn't supported.
