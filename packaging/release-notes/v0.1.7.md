RAWmakase 0.1.7 makes editing and browsing smoother and brings your Lightroom files over in fewer steps. Sliders keep up with the pointer while previews render, saving no longer interrupts editing, and whole folders of profiles, lens profiles and presets can be imported at once. On an Omarchy desktop the interface now follows the desktop's theme from the start.

## New

- **Import whole folders of profiles, lens profiles and presets.** The setup assistant, **Presets › + › Import Folder…**, the Profile menu's **Import profiles from folder…** and the Lens panel's **Import Folder…** scan a folder and its subfolders for DCP profiles and looks, LCP lens profiles or XMP presets. Files already imported are counted instead of duplicated, presets keep their folders as groups, and anything that can't be imported is summarised with the reason. File choosers also accept several files at once.

## Improved

- **Sliders follow the pointer while previews render.** Dragging a slider such as Exposure could stop for up to a second and then jump, whenever a longer render was running on the GPU. The window now keeps drawing while the GPU works.
- **Saving no longer interrupts editing.** Edits are saved in the background shortly after you stop changing them, so a slow or busy disk can't pause the window. Switching photos, catalogs or closing still waits until your edit is saved.
- **Browsing a large catalog stays responsive.** Edited previews now render on two threads of their own, newest request first, and skip photos that have scrolled away. The status bar shows how many are left.
- **Catalog imports show their progress.** Importing or opening a catalog now says what it is doing, in the Library and in the setup assistant.
- **Follows Omarchy's theme by default.** On an Omarchy desktop, RAWmakase uses the desktop's theme and follows it when it changes, until you choose another in **Preferences › Display › Theme**. Other desktops and macOS keep RAWmakase's own greys. If you previously chose **RAWmakase** there on Omarchy, choose it again to keep it.

## Fixed

- **Resizing the window no longer crashes the app.** Toggling a window between tiled and floating in a tiling window manager such as Hyprland could close RAWmakase when the GPU was busy at that moment.

## Requirements

Mac downloads require macOS 15+. Linux packages are for x86_64: Ubuntu 24.04+ or Debian 13+, Fedora 43+, or current Arch Linux. AUR publication remains paused; the Arch package is available directly above. On Apple Silicon Macs, 0.1.6 offers this update in the app.
