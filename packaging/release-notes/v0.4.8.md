RAWmakase 0.4.8 replaces the Navigator with tools that sit on the photo: an Aperture-style magnifier, a zoom slider and a minimap. The Library gains Lightroom-style folder stacks, including Auto-Stack by Capture Time and stacks imported from Lightroom catalogs. Positive Shadows and negative Highlights now follow Camera Raw more closely on most photos, and camera profiles that share a name and camera can each be chosen.

## New

- **Folder stacks.** **Group into Stack** (`Cmd/Ctrl+G`) turns the selected photos into one collapsed stack, with the active photo on top and a count badge. Press `S`, or click the badge, to collapse or expand it. The thumbnail menu's **Stacking** submenu also has **Unstack** (`Cmd/Ctrl+Shift+G`), **Remove from Stack**, **Move to Top of Stack** (`Shift+S`) and **Collapse/Expand All Stacks**. All Photographs and folders show stacks; collections list photos one by one, as in Lightroom. Each change is one Undo step. ([#732](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/732))
- **Auto-Stack by Capture Time…** stacks consecutive photos in the same folder that were taken less than an interval apart, which you set with a slider (up to one hour). The dialog shows how many stacks it would make. ([#732](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/732))
- **Lightroom import brings folder stacks**, with their tops and collapse state. Collection stacks aren't imported. ([#732](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/732))
- **Magnifier.** `Shift+Z`, or the magnifier button in the toolbar, shows a lens that follows the pointer over the fitted photo in Develop and the Library's Loupe. It shows the area under it at 100%, 200% or 400%; choose the level from the toolbar menu or with the mouse wheel. In Develop it shows your edit. ([#739](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/739))
- **Zoom slider and minimap.** Develop's toolbar and the Library Loupe's have a zoom slider from Fit to 1600%. When part of the photo is out of view, a minimap appears over it: click or drag it to move the view, or drag its grip to another corner. `Cmd/Ctrl+=` and `Cmd/Ctrl+−` zoom in and out, stopping at 100% and at Fit; `Cmd/Ctrl+0` zooms to 100% and `Cmd/Ctrl+1` fits the photo. ([#739](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/739))

## Improved

- **Shadows and Highlights closer to Camera Raw.** Positive Shadows and negative Highlights now use a curve computed from each photo's histogram. In our tests against Camera Raw 18.7, Highlights −100 and Shadows +100 got closer on most test sets. Very dark photos with small bright lights can still differ. Other sliders render as before. ([#733](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/733))
- **The histogram is always at the top of Develop's right panel**, in its own card. ([#739](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/739))
- **Side panel headers line up.** Develop's Presets and adjustment section headers now start at the same height, and the gaps between sections are slightly tighter. ([#730](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/730))

## Fixed

- **Camera profiles that share a name and camera can each be chosen.** Before, the Profile menu showed only the first of them. Each now appears, with its file name in the title, for example `Camera Neutral (Z.dcp)`. Exact duplicates are still hidden, and saved edits keep using the same profile. ([#735](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/735), [#734](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/issues/734))

## Removed

- **The Navigator panel**, in Develop and the Library; the magnifier, zoom slider and minimap take its place. **Pinned panels** are also gone. ([#739](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/739))

## Upgrade notes

- Stacks are stored in the catalog with no format change, so earlier releases still open it; they show the photos unstacked.

Thanks to [@adambuchweitz](https://github.com/adambuchweitz) for the camera profile report ([#734](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/issues/734)).
