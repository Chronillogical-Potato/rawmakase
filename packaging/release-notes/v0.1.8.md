RAWmakase 0.1.8 gives the app a new face and a calmer, more focused workspace. It has a new icon, and on macOS 26 it is drawn with Liquid Glass. Photos are now always edited through the Library, and dropping a photo onto the window adds it there. Stepping through the filmstrip is quicker, and the interface no longer jumps around as you move from photo to photo.

## New

- **A new app icon.** A maki roll seen end-on, its salmon centre cut into aperture blades. On macOS 26 the Dock and Finder draw it with Liquid Glass; earlier macOS versions and Linux show the flat version. The Dock now shows RAWmakase's own icon while the app runs, instead of a generic one.
- **Dropped photos join the Library.** Dropping a RAW file onto the window, or passing one on the command line (`rawmakase photo.dng`), adds its folder to the open catalog and opens the photo in Develop.

## Improved

- **Stepping through the filmstrip is faster.** While you edit a photo, the next one in the filmstrip (or the previous one, if you are stepping back) is decoded in the background, so moving to it opens straight away instead of decoding first.
- **A cleaner top bar and toolbar.** Library and Develop are now a switcher like macOS's segmented controls, with the selection sliding between them. The top bar's text and icons are smaller, and the catalog menu sits right beside the window buttons. In Develop, the Navigator column runs the full height of the window, the toolbar starts at the photo, and Export stands out as the main button.
- **Consistent controls throughout.** The flag filter, Navigator zoom, preset filter, Color / Black & White and the mask and retouch options all share the same sliding control, and labels are never cut off.
- **The layout stays still.** Selecting a photo with a colour label, switching to a photo from another camera, or clicking a thumbnail in the filmstrip no longer shifts the rows and panels around. Colour labels in the grid and filmstrip are shown by the cell's tint alone.
- **The histogram describes the whole photo at 100%.** Like Lightroom's, it no longer changes as you zoom and pan.

## Changed

- **Photos are edited through the Library.** Develop's **Open** menu and **⌘O / Ctrl+O** are gone: add a folder to the catalog (or drop a photo onto the window), then pick a photo. Photos you edited outside a catalog in earlier releases keep their edits: when you add their folder, the edits saved beside them (`photo.rawmakase.json`) are brought into the catalog. Those files are left as they are, and edits are no longer saved beside photos.
- **Zoom lives in the Navigator.** The toolbar's Fit / 100% buttons are gone. Use the Navigator's zoom control, or press **Z** and **F**.

## Requirements

Mac downloads require macOS 15+. Linux packages are for x86_64: Ubuntu 24.04+ or Debian 13+, Fedora 43+, or current Arch Linux. AUR publication remains paused; the Arch package is available directly above. On Apple Silicon Macs, 0.1.7 offers this update in the app.
