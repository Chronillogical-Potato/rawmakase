RAWmakase 0.2.1 is mostly a release you shouldn't notice. Over the past days almost the whole app was reorganized: the editing rules, the catalog, file formats, rendering and export now live in separate, clearly bounded parts. Nothing about how you edit changes, but it makes RAWmakase much easier to keep fixing and to extend for years to come. Along the way it fixed real problems: on Linux, photos open in Develop in a fraction of a second again, quitting no longer loses your last edit, a window resize can no longer crash the app, and Texture, strong Vibrance and black & white render closer to Camera Raw in new edits.

A change this size may have introduced a few new issues, even though each step was tested and reviewed. If anything looks or behaves differently from 0.2.0, please [open an issue](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/issues). It really helps.

## TL;DR

- **A rebuilt foundation** for long-term maintenance and new features. Please report anything odd.
- **Photos open in Develop quickly on Linux again**, instead of taking 6–8 seconds.
- **Quitting saves your edit**, however you quit, and no longer hangs on a stalled network drive.
- **Quit asks before cutting off an export** on a Mac, whether from ⌘Q, the Dock or a logout.
- **Resizing a tiled window no longer crashes** while Develop renders on a Mac.
- **Closer to Camera Raw:** Texture, Vibrance beyond ±50 and the black & white mix, in new edits.

## What you can now do

- **Quit while an export runs on a Mac:** press ⌘Q, use Quit in the Dock or log out. RAWmakase asks first, with **Work still pending**, and brings its window to the front even when it was minimized or hidden.
- **Type a value beyond the slider:** click a slider's number, type a value and press Return. Exposure, for example, now takes anything from −8 to +8 EV, as a saved edit may hold.

## Improved

- **A reorganized app.** The editing rules, catalog, Lightroom file formats, control protocol, rendering and export are now separate building blocks with clear responsibilities, and automated checks keep them that way. This took more than a hundred small, reviewed steps ([#216](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/issues/216)). It changes nothing you need to do, and your catalogs and edits stay as they were.
- **Faster Develop on Linux.** With Adobe's lens profiles imported, opening a photo spent 6–8 seconds matching it against every profile. It now takes under 0.1 s on the same machine. The slowdown came with 0.1.15 ([#241](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/241)).
- **Background work leaves Develop alone.** Prefetching the next photo and building edited previews now decode with at most two cores, and run at low priority on Linux and Windows, so they don't slow down the photo you're editing ([#243](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/243)).
- **An idle window stays idle.** RAWmakase used to redraw several times a second while an export, import or autosave was pending, or while the Automation preferences were open. It now redraws only when something actually changes, which uses less power. AI assistants connected over MCP wait for a photo or preview instead of asking every 100 ms ([#277](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/277), [#279](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/279)).
- **Rendering closer to Camera Raw.** These were measured against Camera Raw 18.7 and refitted:
  - **Texture** works on broad, soft detail and on color edges as Camera Raw's does, instead of only sharpening the finest detail. At ±100 it moved about a quarter closer to Camera Raw ([#220](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/220)).
  - **Vibrance from ±50 to ±100** follows Camera Raw's renders. Vibrance −100 now removes about as much color as Camera Raw. Up to ±50 nothing changes ([#219](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/219)).
  - **Black & white** turns each color into the gray Camera Raw gives it, both with the default mix and as you move each color's slider ([#225](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/225)).
- **The setup assistant opens at once.** It no longer freezes for several seconds reading RAW files, and Leica cameras now find their Adobe profiles ([#214](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/214)).

## Fixed

- **Quitting no longer loses your last edit.** On a Mac, ⌘Q, Quit in the Dock and logging out closed the app without saving an edit that autosave hadn't written yet ([#269](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/269)).
- **Quit asks before cutting off work on a Mac.** ⌘Q and the app menu's Quit, then Quit from the Dock and logging out, now go through the same check as closing the window. They save your edit first, and stop to ask while an export or Sync Settings runs or when the edit can't be saved. The question appears in front even when the window is minimized ([#300](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/300), [#317](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/317), [#319](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/319), [#325](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/325)).
- **Quitting no longer hangs on a stalled catalog.** A catalog on a stalled network drive used to hold quitting up for as long as it stalled. Now, if the catalog doesn't answer within about 3 seconds, the window stays open and says so, with **Close without saving**. An export cut off at quit no longer leaves a temporary file next to the exported photos ([#338](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/338)).
- **Resizing a tiled window no longer crashes on a Mac.** Resizing a window snapped to part of the screen could abort RAWmakase while Develop rendered a preview ([#215](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/215)).
- **Undo after removing a virtual copy.** Removing a virtual copy and then making a new one could make Undo change the new copy's rating or settings. Undo now forgets photos you removed ([#324](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/324)).
- **Values beyond a slider are kept.** An imported Exposure of +6 EV no longer drops to +5 as soon as Develop shows it, or when you turn a dial or press an arrow key over it ([#222](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/222), [#326](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/326)).
- **Autosave recovers from an internal failure.** If a save failed unexpectedly, autosave used to stop for that photo until you moved on. It now says so and tries again ([#218](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/218)).

## Changed

- **Rendering changes apply to new edits only.** The new Texture, Vibrance and black & white apply to new edits, and to Lightroom edits and presets that set them. Photos you edited before keep the look they had. A mask's Texture still uses the earlier rendering.

## Requirements

Mac downloads require macOS 15+. Windows downloads are for 64-bit Windows 10 and later (x86_64). Linux packages are for x86_64 and ARM (aarch64): Ubuntu 24.04+ or Debian 13+, or Fedora 43+. The Arch Linux package is x86_64. AUR publication remains paused, so get the Arch package from the link above. Homebrew users update with `brew upgrade --cask rawmakase`. On Apple Silicon Macs and with the Windows installer, 0.2.0 offers this update in the app.

0.2.0 can still open a catalog used by 0.2.1. It renders edits made in 0.2.1 with its older Texture, Vibrance and black & white.
