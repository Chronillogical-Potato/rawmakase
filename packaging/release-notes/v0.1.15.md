RAWmakase 0.1.15 brings more of Lightroom's Develop tools: Point Color, color grading wheels, Before/After and Reference View, the Targeted Adjustment Tool, panel switches and Solo Mode, saved point curves and preset Amount. You can now also edit with a MIDI controller such as the Loupedeck+, or from scripts and AI assistants over MCP. New edits render much closer to Camera Raw: tone curve, Contrast, Whites, color grading, the Color Mixer, sharpening, grain, Heal and Clone edges, manual lens vignetting and out-of-gamut colors were each measured against Camera Raw 18.7 and refitted. Unedited photos from 180 cameras now open as bright as in Lightroom.

## TL;DR: new in Develop

Shortcuts are shown as on a Mac. On Windows and Linux, use Ctrl for ⌘ and Alt for ⌥.

- **Adjust one exact color:** Color Mixer › **Point Color**, click a color in the photo with the dropper, then move Hue, Sat and Lum Shift. **Visualize Range** shows what it selects.
- **Grade with color wheels:** drag the puck in Color Grading's wheels. **⇧** keeps to hue or saturation, **⌘** moves finely, double-click resets.
- **Compare with the original:** **Y** for Before/After side by side, **⌥Y** top and bottom, **⇧Y** split. The toolbar's **Before / After** menu copies or swaps settings between the sides.
- **Match a look to another photo:** **⇧R** for Reference View, then drag a photo from the filmstrip onto the Reference side.
- **Adjust by dragging on the photo:** the target button in Tone Curve, Color Mixer or B&W (**⌥⇧⌘T**, **H**, **S**, **L**, **G**), then drag up or down over a color or tone.
- **Turn a panel off without losing its settings:** the switch in its header.
- **Keep only one panel open:** right-click a panel header › **Solo Mode**.
- **Nudge a slider from the keyboard:** hover it and press **↑** or **↓** (**⇧** for ten steps).
- **Tone down or boost a preset:** after applying it, drag **Amount** at the top of the Presets panel (0–200%).
- **Pick or save a point curve:** Tone Curve › **Point Curve** menu: Linear, Medium or Strong Contrast, your own, or **Save…**.
- **Choose the lens profile yourself:** Lens Corrections › Profile › **Setup**, **Make**, **Model** and **Profile**.
- **Start new photos with the camera's own look:** Preferences › Profiles & Presets › Raw Defaults › **Camera Settings**.
- **Read pixel values:** hover over the photo; **R G B %** shows under the histogram.
- **Show photo info over the photo:** **I** cycles Info 1, Info 2 and off.
- **Size a brush with the mouse:** scroll over the photo with Remove, a mask brush or Red Eye. **⇧**-scroll changes the feather.
- **Edit with a Loupedeck+ or other MIDI controller:** Preferences › Automation › MIDI devices › **Add device** (Mac and Windows).
- **Let an AI assistant or script edit:** Preferences › Automation › Scripts and AI › **Allow local scripts and applications**, then connect your MCP client to `rawmakase mcp` ([setup guide](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/blob/v0.1.15/docs/mcp.md)).

## New

- **Point Color.** The Color Mixer has Lightroom's Mixer and Point Color tabs. The dropper samples a color as rendered and adds up to eight swatches, each with Hue, Saturation and Luminance shifts, Variance, Range and adjustable hue, saturation and luminance ranges. Lightroom's Point Color edits import, render and write back to XMP, and travel with the Color group in Copy, Sync and presets ([#144](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/144), [#145](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/145)).
- **Color grading wheels.** Color Grading has Lightroom's 3-Way view and a large wheel for Shadows, Midtones, Highlights or Global, with Luminance under each wheel and Blending and Balance in every view ([#149](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/149)).
- **Before/After views.** Before shows beside the edit, left and right, top and bottom, or split, with shared zoom and pan. You can copy Before to After, After to Before, or swap them, and set Before from a History step or snapshot. Backslash still shows Before alone ([#150](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/150)).
- **Reference View.** Shows another photo, with its own saved edit, beside the one you're editing. It stays while you move between photos, and **Lock** keeps it after you leave Develop ([#159](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/159)).
- **Targeted Adjustment Tool.** Dragging on the photo moves the Tone Curve region, the Color Mixer bands or the B&W mix that affect the pixel under the pointer, as one History step ([#160](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/160)).
- **Panel switches and Solo Mode.** Tone Curve, Color Mixer, Color Grading, Detail, Lens Corrections, Transform, Effects and Calibration have Lightroom's on/off switch. Each toggle is a History step and is saved with the edit. Solo Mode keeps one panel open on each side ([#155](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/155)).
- **Preset Amount.** Presets that support it can be applied at 0–200%. Built-in presets and your own scalable presets offer it ([#143](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/143)).
- **Point Curve menu.** Lightroom's Linear, Medium Contrast and Strong Contrast curves, plus curves you save. Saved curves use the file layout that Lightroom and Camera Raw use ([#157](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/157)).
- **Choose a lens profile.** Lens Corrections has Lightroom's Setup, Make, Model and Profile menus, listing the imported profiles that fit the camera, including profiles for adapted lenses. Lightroom edits now render with the profile they name, and say so when it isn't imported ([#151](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/151)).
- **Camera Settings raw default.** New photos can start from the camera-matching profile (Camera Standard, or PROVIA/Standard on Fujifilm), as Lightroom's Camera Settings does. It falls back to Adobe Default, with a note, when that profile isn't imported ([#175](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/175)).
- **RGB readout and info overlay.** Develop shows the R, G and B values under the pointer in Lightroom's percentages, and **I** shows the same photo info overlay as the Library's Loupe ([#161](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/161)).
- **MIDI controllers, scripts and MCP.** Preferences › Automation adds MIDI devices, with a Loupedeck+ profile or your own mappings, as well as a local control socket for scripts. `rawmakase mcp` lets local MCP clients such as Codex find photos, adjust settings and curves, run Auto, look at previews, undo, save and export, all in the running app. External control is off until you enable it. MIDI works on macOS and Windows. See [MIDI](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/blob/v0.1.15/docs/midi.md), [MCP](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/blob/v0.1.15/docs/mcp.md) and [scripting](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/blob/v0.1.15/docs/automation.md) ([#179](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/179)).
- **New cameras.** The Sony A7 V opens, including its default compressed raws, and the Sony A1 II and Canon EOS R50 V use their own color matrices. The Hasselblad X2D II 100C is recognized. Arch packages use the system LibRaw, so they get these cameras once Arch's LibRaw does ([#178](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/178)).

## Improved

- **Unedited photos open as bright as in Lightroom.** A table of 180 cameras gives each one Camera Raw's baseline exposure. Many Canon, Nikon and Sony bodies used to open about 0.3 EV darker, and up to 0.66 EV darker. On 156 sample raws, 142 now open within ±0.1 EV of Camera Raw. Canon photos shot with Highlight Tone Priority and Fujifilm photos at DR200 or DR400 no longer open a stop dark. Fujifilm photos at extended low ISO no longer open a stop bright ([#172](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/172), [#177](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/177), [#189](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/189), [#194](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/194)).
- **Rendering much closer to Camera Raw.** These were measured against Camera Raw 18.7 on synthetic charts and refitted:
  - Tone Curve regions, also when a look carries its own curve ([#164](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/164), [#183](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/183), [#184](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/184)).
  - Contrast now pivots where each photo needs it ([#165](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/165)), and positive Whites follow the photo's highlights ([#170](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/170)).
  - Color Grading at every Blending and Balance, legacy split toning included ([#168](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/168)), and all eight Color Mixer bands, especially Blue and Purple ([#188](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/188)).
  - Colors pushed outside sRGB clip per channel instead of fading toward grey ([#176](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/176)).
  - Sharpening starts from Lightroom's raw defaults (Amount 40, Radius 1.0, Detail 25) and sharpens fine detail as strongly as Camera Raw, with smaller halos on strong edges ([#180](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/180)).
  - Grain is as strong as Camera Raw's and scales with the photo's size ([#190](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/190)).
  - Heal and Clone spots feather as Camera Raw's do, staying solid further out ([#191](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/191)).

  On the 369-case chart, the mean color difference from Camera Raw fell from 1.62 to 1.29 ΔE00 with the gamut change alone.
- **Auto tone follows Lightroom's current Auto.** Auto sets Contrast to about +6 as Lightroom does now, sets Saturation by how colorful the photo is, and is no longer biased on Shadows and Blacks ([#162](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/162)).
- **Camera-matching profiles keep their shadows.** Camera Standard and Adobe's other camera-matching profiles no longer crush shadows compared with Lightroom ([#173](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/173)).
- **Lightroom-like controls.** Remove brush strokes show as a clean outline, and sliders that start at zero fill from the left ([#146](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/146), [#147](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/147)).
- **Quieter Lightroom imports.** Lightroom 15's Glow, HDR and Distraction Removal settings are no longer reported as unsupported when they do nothing ([#171](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/171)).

## Changed

- **Rendering changes apply to new edits only.** The new tone curve, Contrast, Whites, Color Grading, Color Mixer, gamut clipping, sharpening, grain, Heal and Clone edges and manual vignetting apply to new edits and new Lightroom imports. Photos you edited before keep the look they had. Sharpening and grain switch to the new rendering once an XMP or preset sets them, or when grain is first added. The new camera exposures also apply only to photos without a saved edit.
- **Manual lens Vignetting now goes the right way.** Negative values darken the corners, as in Lightroom, and cropping no longer changes the falloff. Edits made before keep their old vignetting ([#167](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/167)).
- **Red Eye circles are sized before you click.** Dragging no longer sizes the circle. Use the mouse wheel or **[** and **]**, as in Lightroom ([#142](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/142)).

## Fixed

- **Filmstrip's last photo.** The filmstrip now scrolls far enough to show its last photo in full, and its header stays inside the window ([#148](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/148)).
- **Importing a folder of lens profiles from the command line.** `rawmakase import-lens-profiles` imports each file on its own and lists the files it skipped, instead of stopping at the first unusable one ([#182](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/182)).

## Requirements

Mac downloads require macOS 15+. Windows downloads are for 64-bit Windows 10 and later (x86_64). Linux packages are for x86_64 and ARM (aarch64): Ubuntu 24.04+ or Debian 13+, or Fedora 43+. The Arch Linux package is x86_64. AUR publication remains paused, so get the Arch package from the link above. Homebrew users update with `brew upgrade --cask rawmakase`. On Apple Silicon Macs and with the Windows installer, 0.1.14 offers this update in the app.

0.1.14 can still open a catalog used by 0.1.15 and keeps settings it doesn't know. It renders edits made in 0.1.15 with its older operators, and without their Point Color or chosen lens profile.
