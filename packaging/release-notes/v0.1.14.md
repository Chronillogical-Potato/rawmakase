RAWmakase 0.1.14 is a big step towards Lightroom's Develop module. You can copy, paste and sync chosen settings across photos, keep History and Snapshots with each photo, make your own presets and raw defaults, and use Lightroom's Treatment, Profile Amount, Constrain Crop, straighten ruler, Guided Upright and Red Eye tools. Far more Lightroom edits now import as they look in Lightroom, including the Artistic, Vintage and Modern creative profiles, and Auto tone and post-crop vignettes are much closer to Lightroom's.

## TL;DR: new in Develop

Shortcuts are shown as on a Mac. On Windows and Linux, use Ctrl for ⌘ and Alt for ⌥.

- **Copy only some settings:** **⇧⌘C** and tick the groups you want, then **⇧⌘V** to paste.
- **Paste from the previous photo:** **⌥⌘V**.
- **Sync settings to many photos:** ⌘-click or ⇧-click photos in the filmstrip, then **⇧⌘S**.
- **Match exposure across photos:** select them in the filmstrip, then **⌥⇧⌘M** (Settings › Match Total Exposures).
- **Take a snapshot:** **+** on the Snapshots panel. Click one to go back to it; right-click to update, rename or delete.
- **Come back to History later:** it is saved with the photo and returns when you reopen it, even after a restart.
- **Make your own preset:** **⇧⌘N** (Settings › New Develop Preset…). Right-click it in the Presets panel to update, rename or delete.
- **Choose what new photos start from:** Preferences › Profiles & Presets › Raw Defaults, for all cameras or per camera.
- **Switch to black & white:** **V**, or Basic › Treatment. Press **Auto** in the B&W panel for an automatic mix.
- **Tone down or boost a creative profile:** the **Amount** slider under Profile (0–200%).
- **Keep colours while adding curve contrast:** Tone Curve › Point › Refine › **Saturation**.
- **Fix barrel or pincushion distortion by hand:** Lens Corrections › Distortion › **Amount**.
- **Crop out white edges after Transform:** tick **Constrain Crop** in the Transform panel.
- **Level a horizon:** in Crop (**R**), ⌘-drag along it, or press **Ruler**. **Auto** levels it for you.
- **Swap crop orientation:** **X**.
- **Crop guides:** **O** cycles Grid, Thirds, Diagonal, Triangle, Golden Ratio and Golden Spiral; **⇧O** turns them; the Crop panel's **Overlay** menu sets when they show.
- **Straighten with guides:** **⇧T** (or Transform › Upright › Guided), then draw 2–4 lines along edges that should be vertical or horizontal. **Delete** removes the selected one; **Clear** removes all.
- **Remove red eye:** pick the **Red Eye** tool next to Remove and drag from the centre of the eye outwards.
- **Fix glowing pet eyes:** in the Red Eye tool set **Type** to **Pet Eye**; drag the catchlight circle to place it.
- **See shadow or highlight clipping on its own:** click the histogram's left or right triangle, or hover over it. **J** toggles both.
- **Adjust tones from the histogram:** drag sideways in it to move Blacks, Shadows, Exposure, Highlights or Whites.

## New

- **Copy Settings, Paste from Previous and Sync.** Copy Settings opens Lightroom's dialog of setting groups, and remembers your choice. Paste and Sync work out each photo's own white balance, camera profile and exposure baseline, so settings move correctly between cameras. Sync saves all photos at once, adds a step to each History, reports photos it can't read, and one **⌘Z** undoes it everywhere ([#105](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/105), [#108](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/108), [#109](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/109)).
- **Match Total Exposures.** Evens out a bracketed or mixed series from each photo's aperture, shutter speed and ISO, so each ends up as bright as the open photo ([#118](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/118)).
- **History that lasts, and Snapshots.** History is saved with each photo and virtual copy, including undone steps you can still redo. Snapshots sit above History as in Lightroom, and snapshots in an imported Lightroom catalog come across ([#103](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/103), [#111](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/111)).
- **Your own Develop presets.** New Develop Preset… saves the chosen setting groups as a Lightroom-format XMP preset in your user presets, under a group you name. It shows in the Presets panel at once. **Save/Load Preset File…** still handle RAWmakase's older JSON presets ([#117](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/117)).
- **Raw Defaults.** Choose Adobe Default, RAWmakase Default or any Develop preset as the starting point for unedited photos, with overrides per camera. Reset, Before, Sync and previews use them. Adobe Default stays the default, so nothing changes until you pick something ([#120](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/120)).
- **Treatment and Auto black & white mix.** Basic has Lightroom's Color / Black & White switch. The first conversion applies an automatic mix estimated from the photo, which you can turn off under Preferences › Profiles & Presets. Choosing a black & white profile switches the Treatment with it ([#135](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/135)).
- **Profile Amount.** Lightroom's creative profiles can be applied at 0–200%, and Lightroom's B&W and Modern 01 profiles now import ([#134](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/134)).
- **Artistic, Vintage and Modern profiles.** Lightroom's creative profiles built on colour tables, the settings some of them carry, and Adobe's camera-matching profiles for Nikon, Panasonic, Sigma and others now import and render. Fujifilm's film-simulation profiles still can't, because Adobe keeps their tables outside the file ([#137](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/137), [#139](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/139)).
- **Refine Saturation and manual lens Distortion,** with their own sliders, rendered as Camera Raw does ([#112](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/112), [#113](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/113), [#115](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/115)).
- **Constrain Crop.** Shrinks the crop, keeping its aspect, until no white is left from Upright, Transform or Distortion ([#119](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/119)).
- **Crop tools.** The straighten ruler, Auto straighten, **X** to swap the aspect, and Lightroom's six crop overlays. Rotate and Flip now keep the crop on the same part of the photo ([#123](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/123), [#132](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/132)).
- **Guided Upright.** Draw up to four guides with a magnifying loupe and the correction is solved as you go. Guides are read from and written to XMP as Camera Raw stores them. Copy and Sync gain **Upright Transforms** to copy a solved correction exactly ([#133](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/133)).
- **Red Eye and Pet Eye.** The new tool finds the pupil in the circle you drag, with Pupil Size and Darken for red eyes, and a movable catchlight for pet eyes. Lightroom's red eye and pet eye corrections import ([#136](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/136), [#138](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/138)).
- **Histogram controls.** Separate shadow and highlight clipping warnings, and dragging in the histogram to move the tone sliders, one History step per drag ([#121](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/121), [#122](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/122)).

## Improved

- **Auto tone is much closer to Lightroom's.** Auto now measures the photo before your other adjustments, as Lightroom does, and its six tone sliders were fitted to Lightroom's own Auto results. It also sets Vibrance, as Lightroom's Auto does, and runs one render instead of 10–20 ([#94](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/94), [#107](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/107)).
- **More Lightroom edits import intact.** Panels Lightroom switched off now render as off. Named white balance (Daylight, Tungsten…), Paint Overlay vignettes, Auto black & white mix and per-channel point curves import as Lightroom shows them, instead of failing or being dropped ([#98](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/98), [#99](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/99), [#101](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/101), [#102](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/102), [#114](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/114), [#116](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/116)).
- **Post-crop vignettes match Camera Raw.** Highlight Priority, Color Priority and Paint Overlay each render as Camera Raw does, with its mask shape. On our synthetic chart they went from up to 18 mean ΔE00 from Camera Raw to under 3 ([#106](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/106)).
- **Lens corrections without the Adobe profile.** When a Lightroom edit asks for profile corrections and the Adobe lens profile isn't imported, the camera's built-in correction is used, and the import and Lens Corrections panel say so ([#110](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/110)).
- **One filmstrip everywhere.** A single filmstrip runs along the bottom in Grid, Loupe, Compare, Survey and Develop, keeping its scroll position as you switch ([#92](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/92)).
- **Faster masked edits.** Rendering masked Shadows/Highlights took about 23% less time in a CPU benchmark on an Apple M1 Pro (thanks @lukewalker2010, [#129](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/129)).

## Changed

- **Existing vignettes and black & white mixes look a little different.** Because both now follow Camera Raw more closely, a saved post-crop vignette is lighter inside the frame, and a non-zero black & white mix renders slightly differently ([#106](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/106), [#135](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/135)).

## Fixed

- **Panel resets.** Double-clicking Green Defringe's hue range resets it to Green's own range, and resetting Effects resets every vignette and grain control ([#100](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/100)).
- **Leaving a photo mid-drag.** Pressing Left or Right while still dragging a slider now records that change as its own History step ([#131](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/131)).

## Requirements

Mac downloads require macOS 15+. Windows downloads are for 64-bit Windows 10 and later (x86_64). Linux packages are for x86_64 and ARM (aarch64): Ubuntu 24.04+ or Debian 13+, or Fedora 43+. The Arch Linux package is x86_64. AUR publication remains paused, so get the Arch package from the link above. Homebrew users update with `brew upgrade --cask rawmakase`. On Apple Silicon Macs and with the Windows installer, 0.1.13 offers this update in the app.

0.1.13 can still open a catalog used by 0.1.14, without its History and Snapshots. It refuses the edits of photos that use a Profile Amount, one of the newly supported creative profiles or a Paint Overlay vignette.
