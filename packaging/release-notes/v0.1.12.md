RAWmakase 0.1.12 brings the Library much closer to Lightroom for culling. You can select many photos and rate, flag or label them together, and view one large in the **Loupe** with 1:1 zoom. **Compare** shows two side by side and **Survey** shows several at once. Other additions are the **Quick Collection**, Lightroom's attribute filters and sort orders, and grid cell styles. Imported collections can be browsed, and camera and exposure details are shown for every photo. Undo now covers Library and Develop changes in one sequence, and the Library remembers how you left it.

## New

- **Select several photos.** In the grid, **Shift+click** or **Shift+arrows** select a range, and **⌘-click** adds or removes a photo. **⌘A** and **⌘D** select all or none, and **/** deselects the active photo. Rating, flag and label keys apply to every selected photo at once, as in Lightroom ([#48](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/48)).
- **Loupe.** **E**, **Return** or a double-click shows the active photo large, with the filmstrip below. **G**, **Esc** or a double-click goes back to the grid. A RAW is drawn by Develop's own renderer, with its edits. A click, **Z** or the Navigator zooms to 1:1 and beyond, and **⌘= / ⌘-** step the zoom as in Develop. JPEG, TIFF and PNG files load only the part in view. **I** cycles Lightroom's two info overlays ([#50](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/50), [#51](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/51), [#54](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/54)).
- **Compare (C).** The active photo (the select) is shown beside a candidate, both rendered with their edits at full size. **Left/Right** change the candidate, **Up** makes it the select, and **Down** swaps the two. A click makes either photo active for rating and flag keys ([#58](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/58), [#60](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/60)).
- **Survey (N).** Every selected photo is shown at once, as large as the view allows. A click makes a photo active, the arrows move between photos, and a photo's **×** or **⌘-click** takes it out. Survey shows up to 48 photos at a time ([#61](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/61)).
- **View buttons.** Grid, Loupe, Compare and Survey each have a button in the toolbar under the photos, with their key shown on hover.
- **Quick Collection.** **B** adds the selected photos to the Quick Collection or takes them out, and a dot marks them in the grid. **⌘B** shows it and **⌘⇧B** clears it. It's listed in the Catalog panel ([#56](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/56)).
- **Collections from Lightroom.** A Collections panel lists the collection sets and collections from an imported Lightroom catalog, with counts. The filter bar still applies inside a collection. Smart collections aren't shown yet ([#52](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/52)).
- **Photo info.** The Metadata panel shows each photo's dimensions, exposure, focal length, ISO, camera and lens. Hovering a grid thumbnail shows its name, capture time and dimensions. Photos imported from Lightroom take this from the catalog, and photos added from folders have it read in the background ([#55](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/55)).
- **Grid cell styles.** **J**, or the menu in the grid toolbar, switches between compact cells, expanded cells with a line of details (dimensions, capture date and format), and photos only ([#63](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/63)).
- **Sort orders.** The grid toolbar's **Sort** menu offers Capture Time, Added Order, Edit Time, Rating, Pick, Label Color, Label Text, File Name, File Extension and Aspect Ratio. File names sort as you'd read them (IMG_9 before IMG_10), and the arrow reverses any order ([#64](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/64)).
- **Keyboard shortcuts sheet.** **⌘/**, or the keyboard button in the top bar, shows every shortcut, grouped as in Lightroom.

## Improved

- **Lightroom's attribute filters.** Flags and color labels now combine, for example to show everything but rejects or several labels at once. There are **No label** and **Other labels** choices. The rating compares with ≥, ≤ or =, and there's an **Unrated photos only** choice. **Kind** shows masters or virtual copies, and **⌘L** turns the filters off and back on without losing them ([#57](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/57)).
- **One undo across Library and Develop.** **⌘Z** and **⌘⇧Z** undo and redo ratings, flags, labels, Quick Collection changes and develop edits in the order you made them, from either module ([#49](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/49)).
- **The Library opens as you left it.** The view, the filters, the sort, the cell style and the thumbnail size are kept across launches ([#65](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/65)).
- **Capture times for folder photos.** Photos added from folders now sort by when they were taken. The capture time is read at import, and in the background for photos added before ([#53](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/53)).
- **Smoother slider drags with many presets.** The Develop Presets panel builds its list once instead of on every frame. With about 600 imported presets, this cut the panel's share of each frame from around 0.5 ms to 0.05 ms in our test ([#59](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/59)).

## Requirements

Mac downloads require macOS 15+. Windows downloads are for 64-bit Windows 10 and later (x86_64). Linux packages are for x86_64 and ARM (aarch64): Ubuntu 24.04+ or Debian 13+, or Fedora 43+. The Arch Linux package is x86_64. AUR publication remains paused, so get the Arch package from the link above. Homebrew users update with `brew upgrade --cask rawmakase`. On Apple Silicon Macs and with the Windows installer, 0.1.11 offers this update in the app.
