RAWmakase 0.1.13 adds photo metadata and watermarks. You can give photos a title, caption, creator, copyright and keywords in the Library, read them from XMP sidecars (such as digiKam's) and Lightroom catalogs, and export them with Lightroom's **Include** choices. Exports can now carry a text or image watermark, designed in a Watermark Editor like Lightroom's. DNGs without an embedded camera profile now render in proper color, and Develop says why a photo can't be opened instead of doing nothing.

## New

- **Edit photo metadata.** The Library's Metadata panel has **Title**, **Caption**, **Creator**, **Copyright** and **GPS** rows, as in Lightroom. Edits apply to every selected photo, and a field shows **< mixed >** when they differ. Creator holds several names in order, added with **+**. Every change is one **⌘Z** step ([#74](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/74)).
- **Keywording.** The Keywording panel lists the keywords of the selected photos, marking with **\*** those only some of them have, and **×** removes one. New keywords are typed as in Lightroom, separated by commas, with **Child < Parent** for a hierarchy ([#74](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/74)).
- **Metadata from XMP sidecars.** **Add photo folder** reads each new photo's XMP sidecar (`IMG_1234.NEF.xmp`, as digiKam writes it, or `IMG_1234.xmp`) and, for JPEG and TIFF, the XMP inside the file: title, caption, creators, copyright, keywords, rating, color label, pick, capture time and location. For photos already in the catalog, **Read Metadata from Files…** in the thumbnail menu does the same, after asking, since it replaces your edits ([#77](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/77)). RAWmakase reads sidecars but doesn't write them.
- **Metadata from Lightroom catalogs.** Title, caption, creator, copyright, GPS and capture time now come across from an imported Lightroom catalog. Catalogs imported before read them once when opened, without changing anything you've set ([#77](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/77)).
- **Default Creator and Copyright.** **Preferences > Catalog > Metadata defaults** fills in a creator and copyright for photos added from folders that don't already have one in the file or its sidecar ([#79](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/79)).
- **Watermarks.** The Export dialog has a **Watermarking** section with **Simple Copyright Watermark**, which uses the photo's copyright, and your own saved watermarks. **Edit Watermarks…** opens the **Watermark Editor**, which previews the mark on the photo: text in any installed font with color and shadow, or a PNG or JPEG image, with opacity, size, inset, anchor and rotation ([#78](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/78), [#90](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/90)).

## Improved

- **Lightroom's Include choices for export metadata.** The Export dialog's Metadata section offers Copyright Only, Copyright & Contact Info Only, All Except Camera Raw Info, All Except Camera & Camera Raw Info and All Metadata, with **Remove Location Info**. Exports now carry the catalog's title, caption, creators, copyright, capture time and location. Your previous export settings open as **Custom**, with the same output as before ([#75](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/75)).
- **Keywords keep their shape on export.** Keyword parents are written with the full hierarchy, and a keyword containing a comma, such as "Smith, John", stays one keyword ([#75](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/75)).
- **Matrix-only DNGs render in color.** A DNG with color matrices but no embedded camera profile was rendered with no profile at all. It now uses the file's own color matrix, which brought our synthetic test chart from 13.0 to 1.1 mean ΔE00 against Camera Raw (thanks @lukewalker2010, [#70](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/70)).
- **Develop says why a photo can't be opened.** For an offline photo or a non-RAW file, Open in Develop is greyed out and shows the reason, and pressing **D** explains it in a dialog instead of appearing to do nothing ([#89](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/89)).
- **Safer imports.** Imported presets and profiles are now written so they survive a power loss, and a damaged Lightroom catalog can no longer make the app run out of memory while reading develop history (thanks @lukewalker2010, [#66](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/66), [#67](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/67)).

## Fixed

- **Large XMP in JPEG exports.** A JPEG export no longer fails with "XMP is too large for a JPEG segment" when a long develop history or many keywords make the metadata exceed 64 KB. The extra XMP is written as ExtendedXMP, as Adobe's apps do ([#76](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/76)).
- **The filmstrip follows the photo.** After browsing to a photo in the Loupe and opening it in Develop, Develop's filmstrip now scrolls to it. The same holds for every filmstrip (reported by @crmne in [#85](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/issues/85), [#86](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/86)).

## Requirements

Mac downloads require macOS 15+. Windows downloads are for 64-bit Windows 10 and later (x86_64). Linux packages are for x86_64 and ARM (aarch64): Ubuntu 24.04+ or Debian 13+, or Fedora 43+. The Arch Linux package is x86_64. AUR publication remains paused, so get the Arch package from the link above. Homebrew users update with `brew upgrade --cask rawmakase`. On Apple Silicon Macs and with the Windows installer, 0.1.12 offers this update in the app. A catalog used by 0.1.13 can still be opened by 0.1.12, which ignores the new metadata.
