RAWmakase 0.1.9 comes to Windows and to 64-bit ARM Linux, and it is ready to use from the first launch: it now has camera profiles and presets of its own, so there is nothing to import before you start editing. A new **Auto** button sets white balance and tone in one click, and the white balance picker now shows a Lightroom-style loupe.

## New

- **RAWmakase for Windows.** A Windows 10+ installer that sets the app up for your user account, with no administrator rights needed, plus a zip for those who prefer to unpack it themselves. Installed copies update themselves from the next release on. The downloads are not code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen asks for confirmation the first time you run them.
- **Linux on 64-bit ARM.** Debian/Ubuntu and Fedora packages and the Linux archive now come in an ARM (aarch64) version as well, for machines like Apple Silicon Macs running Asahi Linux. Arch Linux ARM users can build the package from the included recipe.
- **Auto white balance and tone.** The **Auto** button at the top of the Basic panel (**⌘⇧U / Ctrl+Shift+U**) sets white balance, Exposure, Contrast, Highlights, Shadows, Whites and Blacks for the photo, and leaves every other setting alone. White balance also has an **Auto** entry of its own. Auto looks only at what is inside your crop, and it is one step in History, so it is easy to undo.
- **RAWmakase's own camera profiles.** Every supported camera now gets two profiles with nothing to import: **RAWmakase Standard**, a faithful starting point, and **RAWmakase Color**, which adds a gentle, pleasing look much as Adobe Color does. New photos start on RAWmakase Color. Photos you have already edited keep the profile you chose, and imported Adobe profiles work as before.
- **26 built-in presets.** Color looks (Warm, Cool, Punch, Muted, Matte), two creative looks, six Black & White treatments, tone curves, grain and vignetting are in the Presets panel from the start, listed before your own. They are ordinary Lightroom presets, so they can be favourited like any other.

## Improved

- **Lightroom edits look right without Adobe's profiles.** A photo edited in Lightroom with Adobe Standard or Adobe Color now falls back to the matching RAWmakase profile (or the DNG's own profile) when Adobe's isn't imported, instead of adding contrast and colour the photographer never chose. A missing creative look, such as Adobe Vivid, is reported on its own and no longer stops the rest of the edit from applying.
- **A white balance picker you can aim.** While picking white balance, the pointer becomes an eyedropper with a magnifying loupe beside it, showing the surrounding pixels and the picked pixel's values, as in Lightroom. The picker now sits at the start of the WB row.
- **A tidier Basic panel.** Auto and a Black & White switch sit at the top of the panel. Slider values have a fixed-width field, so typing or dragging a value like -100 no longer pushes into the slider.

## Fixed

- **The app recovers from an internal error while rendering.** Previously a failure while rendering, loading or making a preview could leave the spinner running and the photo stuck. That step now fails on its own and the next one works.
- **A stalled network drive no longer piles up background work.** RAWmakase checks your drives every few seconds; while one hangs, it now waits for that check instead of starting more.
- **Brushed masks on a different photo draw correctly.** A brush or Auto Mask could reuse the coverage from another photo with the same strokes.
- **Library previews are kept when the catalog is busy.** A good preview is no longer thrown away because the catalog was briefly locked, and failures to save previews are shown in the Library's status line.

## Requirements

Mac downloads require macOS 15+. Windows downloads are for 64-bit Windows 10 and later (x86_64). Linux packages are for x86_64 and ARM (aarch64): Ubuntu 24.04+ or Debian 13+, or Fedora 43+; the Arch Linux package is x86_64. AUR publication remains paused; the Arch package is available directly above. On Apple Silicon Macs, 0.1.8 offers this update in the app.
