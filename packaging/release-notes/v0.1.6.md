RAWmakase 0.1.6 brings the first, experimental versions of spot removal and masks. You can remove dust and blemishes with Heal and Clone, and brighten, darken or recolour parts of a photo with brush, gradient and range masks. Spots and masks from Lightroom now come along when you apply its edits. Both tools are early: they work, but are not yet measured against Lightroom, and details may change.

## New

- **Spot removal (experimental).** Press **Q** or choose **Remove** in the tool strip under the histogram. Click a spot to heal it from a source RAWmakase picks nearby, drag to paint over a larger area, or Cmd-drag (Ctrl-drag on Linux) to choose the source yourself. Drag a spot or its source to move it, press **/** for the next best source, and switch between **Heal** and **Clone**. **Visualize Spots** (**A**) shows fine detail in black and white so dust stands out. Spots are applied to the full-resolution photo before every other edit, so exports keep full quality.
- **Masks (experimental).** Press **Shift+W** or choose **Masking**. Create a **Brush** (**K**), **Linear Gradient** (**M**), **Radial Gradient** (**Shift+M**), **Color Range** (**Shift+J**) or **Luminance Range** mask, then add, subtract or intersect more of them. Each mask has Lightroom's local sliders: Temp, Tint, Exposure, Contrast, Highlights, Shadows, Whites, Blacks, Texture, Clarity, Dehaze, Hue, Saturation, Sharpness, Noise and a colour tint, plus an Amount. Brushes have Size, Feather, Flow, Density, Auto Mask, A/B brushes and an eraser (Option/Alt); **O** shows the selected mask in red.
- **Lightroom spots and masks on import (experimental).** Applying Lightroom edits from an imported catalog or an XMP file now also brings Heal and Clone spots and brush, gradient, radial and luminance range masks with their sliders. Masks that need AI (Subject, Sky, Objects, People, Depth) and colour range masks are listed as not imported. Positions and sizes of spots were checked against Camera Raw; some gradient details are assumptions (see the [parity notes](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/blob/v0.1.6/docs/parity-gaps.md#local-and-finishing-adjustments)).

## Changed

- **Older versions still open your photos.** Spots and masks are saved beside the rest of the edit: in the catalog, or for photos opened directly in a `photo.ARW.rawmakase-local.json` file next to the usual sidecar. RAWmakase 0.1.5 and earlier open every photo normally, just without spots and masks. Edits saved by this version also keep settings added by future versions instead of rejecting them.
- **Presets and Paste Settings leave spots alone,** as in Lightroom. Presets you save can include masks.

## Known limitations

Spot removal and masks are early. The local sliders, feathering, the automatic source choice and the heal itself are RAWmakase's own and have not been compared with Lightroom numerically. The AI Remove mode, AI masks, local Moiré, Defringe and tone curves are not available yet. While a photo has spots, previews keep an extra full-resolution copy of it in memory.

## Requirements

Mac downloads require macOS 15+. Linux packages are for x86_64: Ubuntu 24.04+ or Debian 13+, Fedora 43+, or current Arch Linux. AUR publication remains paused; the Arch package is available directly above. On Apple Silicon Macs, 0.1.5 offers this update in the app.
