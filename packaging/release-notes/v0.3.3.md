## New

- **Nikon High Efficiency NEFs open and edit (experimental)** ([#521](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/521), refs [#411](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/issues/411), reported by @kniziol). High Efficiency and High Efficiency★ raws from the Z 9, Z 8, Z f, Z6III, Z5II, Z50II and other bodies used to be refused. RAWmakase now decodes them itself. On every sample we have (28 files from 6 bodies, plus the one attached to #411), the decoded raw data is identical to Adobe DNG Converter's. Nikon doesn't document the format and every sample so far uses one encoder setting, so a file the decoder can't read is refused with an error rather than shown wrong. Please report any that fail.
- **Sync Settings from the Library grid** ([#519](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/519), closes [#445](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/issues/445), requested by @EttVenter). Select the photos, make the edited one active, then press Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+S or click **Sync Settings…** at the foot of the Library's right panel. It works as Sync does in Develop, with one Undo step. The source doesn't need to be open in Develop. If its edit can't be read, nothing is changed.
- **Select several folders in the Folders panel** ([#520](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/520), closes [#508](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/issues/508), requested by @ThomasHabets). Shift-click selects a range of folders; Cmd-click (Ctrl-click on Windows and Linux) adds or removes one. The grid shows the photos of all of them together.
- **Choose the decoded-image cache size** ([#511](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/511), thanks @ThomasHabets). Preferences > Catalog > **Decoded images > Cache size limit** sets how much disk space to keep for decoded RAWs, from 1 GiB up (4 GiB by default). A larger cache reopens more photos without decoding them again. Changes apply without restarting.

## Improved

- **Fujifilm X-Trans fine detail no longer turns green and magenta** ([#518](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/518)). Fine regular patterns, such as louvres on a building, could show a colour cast. X-Trans now has its own demosaic that follows the direction of detail. Opening an X-Trans photo in Develop takes a little longer (about 0.6 s instead of 0.4 s on one Mac).
- **Clipped light sources keep their brightness** ([#513](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/513)). Where only one colour channel was clipped, highlight recovery could pull it below what the sensor recorded, tinting or darkening the brightest areas. On the worst photo tested this was 15 ΔE00 away from Camera Raw; it is now about 1.
- **Sony photos at extended low ISO are no longer a stop too bright** ([#514](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/514)). At ISO 50, 64 and 80 on the A7, A7R, A7R II, A7 III, A7R III, A7 IV and A7R IV, RAWmakase now takes off the stop Lightroom does.
- **Canon sRAW and mRAW match Lightroom's brightness more closely** ([#515](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/515)). They get a default exposure of their own instead of the full-size raw's. Across the tested sRAW and mRAW files, the median difference from Camera Raw fell from 2.6 to 1.1 ΔE00; two files moved slightly further away.
- **Pentax lenses are named and matched to lens profiles** ([#504](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/504), fixes [#501](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/issues/501), thanks @martijnvandijk). Pentax cameras don't write the lens name. RAWmakase now reads the lens code they do write, so the lens shows in the Library and Develop, goes into exports, and its Adobe lens profile is found automatically.

## Fixed

- **Canon Highlight Tone Priority photos are no longer too dark** ([#512](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/512)). Bodies such as the EOS 550D and 60D record Highlight Tone Priority where LibRaw doesn't look, so RAWmakase missed the stop Lightroom adds. The EOS 1500D (2000D, Rebel T7) also gets its own calibration.
- **Fewer magenta casts on newer Canon CR3s** ([#517](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/517), refs [#516](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/issues/516), thanks @akhater). LibRaw misreads the black level of the EOS R6 Mark III, C50, R50 V and PowerShot V1, which could turn high-ISO photos pink. RAWmakase now reads it from the file itself. This hasn't yet been confirmed on the reporter's high-ISO photo.
- **PNGs show their camera, lens, capture time and XMP** ([#509](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/509), thanks @priard). RAWmakase's own PNG exports write this metadata, but it used to be ignored on import. PNGs already in a catalog get their camera, lens and capture time the next time their file is online.

## Upgrade notes

- The rendering changes above apply to existing edits too: affected Sony, Canon, X-Trans and clipped-highlight photos will look different, closer to Lightroom.
- Export settings saved by a future release with a format this one doesn't know now fall back to JPEG instead of losing every other export choice.

## Known limitations

- Nikon Z5II and Z50II photos look flat unless you import their Adobe profile, because LibRaw has no colour matrix for them. This affects lossless files as well as High Efficiency ones.

Mac downloads require macOS 15+. Windows downloads are for 64-bit Windows 10 and later (x86_64 and ARM64). Linux packages are for x86_64 and ARM (aarch64): Ubuntu 24.04+ or Debian 13+, or Fedora 43+.
